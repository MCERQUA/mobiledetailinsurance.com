Louisiana FAQ

Louisiana mobile detailing insurance — frequently asked

Real questions from Louisiana detailers, answered by people who write the policy. None of this is legal advice — your situation may differ.

Does Louisiana require workers' comp for mobile detailing businesses?

add

Louisiana generally requires workers' compensation coverage for businesses with one or more employees, including part-time and seasonal workers. Sole proprietors with no employees are typically exempt. Verify your specific situation with the Louisiana Workforce Commission's Office of Workers' Compensation Administration and a licensed agent before assuming you're exempt.

How does hurricane season affect mobile detailing insurance in Louisiana?

add

Louisiana sits in one of the most hurricane-active corridors in the country. For mobile detailers, that means comprehensive auto, equipment, and inland marine coverage needs to be sized for catastrophic-weather scenarios — a single named storm can total a van and every tool inside it. Some carriers also tighten binding windows once a storm enters the Gulf, so getting coverage written and bound well before hurricane season starts is the standard practice.

What's the minimum commercial auto liability for mobile detailers in Louisiana?

add

Louisiana's statutory minimum auto liability is $15,000 per person / $30,000 per accident bodily injury and $25,000 property damage. Those minimums are low for a commercial mobile rig running New Orleans and Baton Rouge metro routes — most carriers writing detailer policies recommend at least $300,000 combined single limit, and fleet, dealership, and HOA contracts commonly require $1M.

Does Gulf Coast salt air change how my equipment should be insured?

add

Coastal Louisiana salt air accelerates corrosion on metal pressure-washer frames, pump fittings, hose reels, and trailer hardware. Replacement-cost equipment coverage matters more here than in dry inland states — actual-cash-value depreciation on a rusted-out three-year-old pressure washer leaves you well short of what a new one costs. Routine fresh-water rinses and cover storage help, but coverage that pays full replacement is the financial backstop.
