Why State-Specific Coverage Matters

Detailing rules, climate risks, and commercial-vehicle requirements vary by state. We tailor every policy to local conditions.

Licensing & Compliance

Every state has different licensing minimums, bonding rules, and environmental compliance standards for mobile detailers. We map your policy to your state's requirements so you stay legal everywhere you work.

Climate & Regional Risk

Florida humidity, Arizona heat, Northeast freeze-thaw, Pacific salt-air. Your gear and your clients' vehicles face different threats by region. We price coverage around the real risks your state poses.

DMV & Commercial Vehicle Rules

From California USDOT thresholds to NYC commercial parking permits, every state regulates work vehicles differently. Our commercial-auto endorsements match your state's DMV and weight-class rules.
